Fraction Formulas

Understanding the basic fraction rules makes it easier to work with fractions and check your results.

Adding Fractions

To add fractions with different denominators, find a common denominator before adding the numerators.

a/b + c/d = (ad + bc) / bd

Subtracting Fractions

To subtract fractions, use a common denominator and subtract the corresponding numerators.

a/b − c/d = (ad − bc) / bd

Multiplying Fractions

Multiply the numerators together and multiply the denominators together.

a/b × c/d = ac/bd

Dividing Fractions

To divide fractions, multiply the first fraction by the reciprocal of the second fraction.

a/b ÷ c/d = a/b × d/c

Simplifying Fractions

Divide both the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor to obtain the simplest form.

Simplified Fraction = Numerator ÷ GCD / Denominator ÷ GCD

Fraction to Decimal

A fraction can be converted to a decimal by dividing the numerator by the denominator.

Decimal = Numerator ÷ Denominator

Fraction Calculator Examples

Here are some simple examples showing how common fraction calculations work.

Example 1: Simplify 24/36

The greatest common divisor of 24 and 36 is 12.

24 ÷ 12 / 36 ÷ 12 = 2/3

Example 2: Add 1/2 + 1/4

Convert both fractions to a common denominator.

1/2 + 1/4 = 2/4 + 1/4 = 3/4

Example 3: Multiply 2/3 × 3/5

Multiply the numerators and denominators.

2/3 × 3/5 = 6/15 = 2/5

Example 4: Convert 3/4 to a Decimal

Divide the numerator by the denominator.

3 ÷ 4 = 0.75

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a fraction?

A fraction represents a part of a whole and is written using a numerator above a denominator.

How do I simplify a fraction?

Divide both the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor until no further common factor remains.

How do I add two fractions?

If the denominators are different, find a common denominator first, then add the numerators.
