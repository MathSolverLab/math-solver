FORMULAS

Fraction Formulas

Addition

a/b + c/d = (ad + bc) / bd

Subtraction

a/b − c/d = (ad − bc) / bd

Multiplication

a/b × c/d = ac / bd

Division

a/b ÷ c/d = ad / bc

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a fraction?

A fraction represents a part of a whole and is written using a numerator and denominator.

How do I simplify a fraction?

Divide both the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor until the fraction is in its simplest form.

Can I add and subtract fractions?

Yes. The calculator supports addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division of fractions.

Can I convert a fraction to a decimal?

Yes. Enter the numerator and denominator and use the Fraction to Decimal calculator.
