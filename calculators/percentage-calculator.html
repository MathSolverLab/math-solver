Percentage Formula

Percentages express a number as a fraction of 100. The basic percentage formula is:

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100
This formula is useful for finding what percentage one value represents of another.

Finding a Percentage of a Number

To find X% of a number Y, divide X by 100 and multiply the result by Y.

X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y

Percentage Change Formula

Percentage increase or decrease can be calculated by comparing the difference between the original and new values with the original value.

Percentage Change = ((New − Original) ÷ Original) × 100

Percentage Calculator Examples

Here are a few simple examples showing how percentage calculations work.

Example 1: 20% of 150 (20 ÷ 100) × 150 = 30
Example 2: What percentage is 25 of 100? (25 ÷ 100) × 100 = 25%
Example 3: Increase from 100 to 125 ((125 − 100) ÷ 100) × 100 = 25%
Example 4: Decrease from 200 to 150 ((150 − 200) ÷ 200) × 100 = −25%

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. The percentage symbol is %.

How do I calculate a percentage?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply the result by 100. For example, 25 out of 100 is 25%.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide the difference by the original value, and multiply by 100.
