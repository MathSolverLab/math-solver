FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a percentage?

A percentage is a number expressed as a fraction of 100. The symbol % is used to represent percentage.

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply it by the number. For example, 25% of 200 is 50.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide the difference by the original value, and multiply by 100.

Can percentages be negative?

Yes. Negative percentage changes can represent a decrease, while positive percentage changes represent an increase.
