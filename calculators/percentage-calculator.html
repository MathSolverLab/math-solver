Percentage Formula

To find a percentage of a number, use:

Percentage = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

For example, 20% of 150 is:

(20 ÷ 100) × 150 = 30

Examples

What is 25% of 200?

25 ÷ 100 × 200 = 50

What percentage is 30 of 150?

30 ÷ 150 × 100 = 20%

Increase from 100 to 125?

The value increased by 25%.
